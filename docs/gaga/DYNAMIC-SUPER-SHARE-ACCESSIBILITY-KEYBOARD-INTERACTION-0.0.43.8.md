# Dynamic Super Share Accessibility + Keyboard Interaction Contract v0.0.43.8

## Objective

Ensure Dynamic Super Share remains usable through keyboard, assistive technology, and reduced-motion preferences.

## Contracts

- Focus ownership remains explicit.
- Keyboard navigation does not bypass share lifecycle.

- Screen reader labels remain semantic.
- Motion preference is respected.
