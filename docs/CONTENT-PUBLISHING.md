# Content publishing

## Draft

Write and save in EmDash. Saving a draft does not change the public site. Existing published content stays live.

## Preview

Choose **Preview saved draft** to check the saved version. Save again before previewing newer edits. A private preview is not a publication.

## Publish

Choose **Publish saved draft** when ready. The selected version is checked before publication. If somebody changed the article meanwhile, review the newer saved version and try again. Your draft is preserved.

## Production

**Your article is live** means the public page and its discovery/search entries were verified. **Publish pending** means the update needs attention; do not keep pressing Publish. Ask the site administrator for help. Unpublishing removes the public article while retaining its history.

## Structural change

A new kind of page, block, layout or content field may need application work. It is different from editing an article. The site administrator handles that work.

The new controls are staged for review. Live publishing is disabled until the site administrator activates the verified service. Existing drafts are not published by this qualification.
