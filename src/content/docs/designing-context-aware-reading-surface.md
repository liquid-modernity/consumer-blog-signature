A high-end publishing surface should help readers understand where they are, what matters now, and what they can do next without forcing them to learn the interface.

## Context before controls

The global navigation remains stable while contextual rails adapt to the current route. This separation prevents global actions from competing with content-specific navigation.

## Progressive immersion

The main canvas can move from a compact reading measure to wide or full-bleed media. On compact screens, the Gaga Dynamic Notch and semantic Outline Peek preserve reading context while the contextual Gaga Dock stays out of immersive article mode.

## Inclusive interaction

Mouse hover, keyboard focus, touch targets, reduced motion, semantic labels, and screen-reader metadata are treated as one interaction system rather than separate patches.
